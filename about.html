About us

Guided learning in a supportive environment

Welcome to Brilliant Minds, a dynamic learning and enrichment centre located at Downtown Mall, Newtown, where education meets creativity, innovation, and holistic development.

At Brilliant Minds, we believe every child is unique and full of untapped potential. Our purpose is not only to help students excel academically but also to inspire confidence, creativity, discipline, curiosity, and self-expression. We are committed to building an environment where young minds are encouraged to explore, learn, perform, and grow into confident individuals ready to excel in every stage of life.

We offer expert academic coaching in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, designed to strengthen concepts, improve academic performance, and develop strong analytical and problem-solving skills. With experienced faculty, focused mentoring, and personalized attention, we help students build confidence and achieve academic excellence.

Beyond academics, Brilliant Minds-Downtown serves as a platform for talent discovery and personality development through a wide range of extracurricular and skill-enhancement programs. Our carefully designed activities include Chess, Robotics, Arts & Crafts, Bengali Recitation, Vocal Music, Guitar, Flute, Bharatanatyam Dance, and creative learning experiences that nurture imagination, concentration, confidence, discipline, and innovation.

What sets us apart is our belief in holistic growth where learning is not limited to classrooms but extends into creativity, culture, strategic thinking, and life skills. Our experienced mentors and educators are passionate about guiding students with care, encouragement, and individual attention so that every learner feels motivated to achieve their best.

At Brilliant Minds-Downtown, we are not just an institute - we are a community dedicated to shaping confident learners, creative thinkers, and future achievers.

Our mission

At Brilliant Minds-Downtown, our mission is to provide quality education and holistic development opportunities that empower students to excel academically, creatively, and personally. We strive to create a nurturing and inspiring learning environment where every child can build confidence, discover talents, strengthen skills, and achieve their full potential through expert guidance in academics, arts, music, chess, robotics, and cultural activities.

Our vision

Our vision is to become one of the most trusted and preferred learning and activity centres for young minds, fostering academic excellence, creativity, discipline, innovation, and lifelong learning. We aspire to shape confident, skilled, and responsible individuals who are prepared to succeed in academics and life with passion, knowledge, and values.
